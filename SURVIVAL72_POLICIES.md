## 1. RETURN & REFUND POLICY / 退换货政策

### Why this policy:
Your $42-$145 price point is lower than James Brand ($79-$275), but you're in a COD-heavy market where trust is fragile. A **60-day window** is a strong trust signal for GCC customers who can't touch the product before paying. The $0 restocking fee is a competitive differentiator.

### English (for website):

**60-Day No-Questions Return**

We stand behind every module we ship. If you're not satisfied for any reason, return it within 60 days of delivery for a full refund — no restocking fees, no fine print.

**Conditions:**
- Item must be in resalable condition with original packaging
- Gift Box returns accepted only if the inner foam tray is intact
- Return shipping is covered by Survival72 for defective or incorrect items
- For change-of-mind returns, customer covers return shipping

**How to return:**
Email `support@survival72gear.com` with your order number. We'll reply within 12 hours with a prepaid label (defects) or return instructions (change of mind).

**Refund timeline:**
Refunds are processed within 5 business days of receiving your return. COD refunds are credited to the original payment method or via bank transfer.

### 中文（后台参考）：

**60 天无条件退货**

我们对每一件产品负责。不满意？收货 60 天内全额退款，无退货处理费，无隐藏条款。

- 商品需可二次销售，保留原包装
- 礼品盒退货须保证内衬完好
- 因产品缺陷退货由 Survival72 承担运费
- 因个人喜好退货由客户承担运费
- 退货流程：发送订单号至 support@survival72gear.com，12 小时内回复
- COD 退款：退回原支付方式或银行转账

## 3. WARRANTY / 保修政策

### Why this policy:
A 2-year unconditional guarantee beats James Brand's "limited lifetime" in practical terms — it's clear, unambiguous, and covers everything without lawyerspeak. For GCC buyers who value honor and straightforwardness, this is more credible than a vague "lifetime" promise.

### English:

**2-Year Unconditional Guarantee**

Every Survival72 module is covered by a flat, simple guarantee: if it breaks under normal use within 2 years, we replace it. No warranty card. No proof of abuse vs. defect debates. No exceptions.

**What's covered:**
- Manufacturing defects
- Material failure
- Functional breakage under intended use

**What's not covered (and this is the complete list):**
- Loss or theft
- Cosmetic wear (scratches, patina, finish fading)
- Damage from fire, flood, or combat

**How to claim:**
Email `warranty@survival72gear.com` with a photo of the issue. We ship a replacement within 48 hours — before you even return the original.

### 中文：

**2 年无条件质保**

简单的承诺：正常使用下坏了，换新的。

- 覆盖制造缺陷、材料失效、正常使用下的功能损坏
- 不覆盖：丢失/被盗、外观磨损、火灾/水灾/战斗损坏
- 保修流程：发送照片至 warranty@survival72gear.com
- **先发货后回收**：收到申请后 48 小时内寄出替换品

## 4. PRIVACY POLICY / 隐私政策

### Why this policy:
GCC customers are privacy-conscious. Emphasize that data stays local, no third-party sharing, and no marketing data resale. The key reassurance for this market.

### English:

**Your Data, Your Trust**

We collect only what's needed to fulfill your order: name, shipping address, email, and phone number (required for courier contact in GCC).

**We never:**
- Sell or rent your data to anyone
- Share your information with third parties except for order fulfillment (courier, payment processor)
- Store payment details — all transactions are processed by PCI-compliant gateways

**We do:**
- Use your email for order updates and (if opted in) product announcements
- Include an unsubscribe link in every email
- Delete your data upon request within 30 days

### 中文：

- 仅收集订单所需信息（姓名、地址、邮箱、电话）
- 不出售、不出租、不共享客户数据
- 支付信息由 PCI 合规网关处理，不本地存储
- 每封邮件含退订链接
- 可随时申请删除数据，30 天内处理完毕
