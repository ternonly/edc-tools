# Compliance & Platform Policy Updates - April 24, 2026

## 1. Google Search Essentials (SEO)
*   **March 2026 Core Update**: Introduced "Information Gain" as a primary ranking signal. Sites providing unique, first-hand insights rank significantly higher than those summarizing existing web content.
*   **AI Content Restriction**: Massive penalization of e-commerce sites using generic, unedited AI product descriptions. Sites with non-differentiated "thin" content have seen traffic drops of up to 40%.
*   **Affiliate Cleanup**: Stricter enforcement against "affiliate-first" pages. Product reviews must demonstrate physical handling and original testing (crucial for gear review content).

## 2. Instagram Community & Recommendation Guidelines
*   **Originality Boost**: Content detected as non-original (reposts or minimally edited stock footage) is now explicitly suppressed in Reels and Explore.
*   **The "Trustworthiness" Filter**: Recommendations are gated by account "genuineness." Accounts with low engagement-to-follower ratios or suspected automated "pod" activity are ineligible for the Explore page.
*   **Native Automation**: As of March 2026, native post-scheduling and advanced insights are free for all public accounts, reducing the need for high-risk third-party automation tools.

## 3. X (Twitter) Rules
*   **Collection Ads**: New e-commerce ad format launched in March, allowing lifestyle hero images paired with shoppable product thumbnails (ideal for tactical gear sets).
*   **Handle Reclaiming**: New capability to purchase dormant @handles through the X business dashboard.
*   **Automation & Bot Control**: X has enhanced bot detection focused on "Interaction Quality." Repetitive outreach or automated DM campaigns without high response rates lead to immediate "limited write" account restrictions (Error 261).
*   **Brand Safety**: Stricter algorithmic suppression of brands interacting with or adjacent to "high-sensitivity" political content.
