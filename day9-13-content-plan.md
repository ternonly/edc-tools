```
Behind the design.

Hidden magnetic closure. Custom-cut EVA cradles each tool. Matte black rigid cardboard. Olive drab embossed mark.

```

### LinkedIn (21:00 CST · Cole Mercer)
```
Spent 6 weeks designing the box, not the tools.

Why? Because the carrier IS the system. A great kit in a sloppy bag is a slow kit. A precise kit in a precise housing is a kit you'll actually deploy.

Three constraints I worked to:
→ Open in under 2 seconds (one-handed, gloved)
→ Each tool seats and reseats without thought
→ Total weight under 200g for the housing alone

Designing the container is designing the behavior. Always.

#Survival72 #ProductDesign #SystemsThinking #EDC
```
